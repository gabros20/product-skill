# Surface: Website (marketing / content site)

Purpose: Reshape discovery, positioning, PRD, and metrics for a marketing, brand, portfolio, or
content site — where the "product" is a positioning-and-conversion artifact, not a software product
with ongoing usage.

Read when:
- The surface is a marketing site, landing page, campaign microsite, portfolio, or content property.

Skip when:
- The surface is a web/SaaS application with accounts and recurring usage — use
  [App & SaaS](surface-app-saas.md).
- The surface is an internal tool — use [Internal](surface-internal.md).

Inputs:
- Target audience/segment and the page's single job.
- The vertical, if a niche applies (professional services, luxury, SaaS-marketing, healthcare,
  finance, etc.) — proof strategy and register differ by vertical.
- The stakes level (money/health/legal risk) driving the premium↔information dial.

Produces:
- A dial setting (premium ↔ information) and a trust/proof plan feeding [Positioning](positioning.md).
- A conversion-metric set feeding [Metrics](metrics.md).

## Shifts

- **Discovery.** Validate message-market fit and positioning claims, not feature demand. Identify
  the vertical explicitly — proof devices and register are vertical-specific, not generic.
- **Positioning.** Set the premium↔information dial per surface and scroll-depth by stakes: higher
  money/health/legal risk pushes proof-led and conservative (finance, healthcare, law); more
  lifestyle/prestige-driven allows premium-led — but proof always carries equal final weight. Apply
  the three credibility layers (Visual → Content → Social) and E-E-A-T (Experience, Expertise,
  Authority, Trust) as positioning requirements, not decoration.
- **PRD.** Scope trust/proof elements as acceptance criteria, not polish: named case studies
  (Challenge → Solution → Results, with quantified outcomes), credentialed testimonials (full name +
  role + photo), compliance/credential badges. On a redesign of an existing site, state the
  copy-fidelity spectrum (verbatim / preserve-and-tighten / rewrite) up front — it's an SEO-equity
  decision, not a copywriting preference.
- **Metrics.** Success is conversion rate, lead quality, and Attention Ratio (links ÷ intended
  actions — campaign pages target near 1:1), not activation/retention. Tie the north star to the
  funnel stage this page owns (visit→lead vs. lead→customer).
- **Risk register.** Flag content-drift risk when copy is SEO-load-bearing IP, testimonial/review
  authenticity (fabricated proof is FTC-actionable), and vertical-specific proof gaps (e.g. missing
  attorney credentials, missing case-result figures).

**Recommends:** `design` for experience, layout, and visual direction; `marketing` for SEO, GTM, and
campaign execution.
