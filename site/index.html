Route before acting. Pick one job and at most one surface, read only those references — never the whole library. An agentic product always adds its overlay, because it changes the acceptance criteria.

The router is the skill. There is no fixed pipeline to run start-to-finish — each job stands alone and enters where your question is. The animation traces one path; the sections below map the whole surface it routes across.

Own the decision, not the execution

product owns the front of the lifecycle — the decision about what to build and why. Experience, code, and systems belong to sibling skills; product feeds them a validated decision and a clean handoff, and recommends them by name rather than silently taking their work.

product owns

the front of the product lifecycle

  • discovery — validated problems, JTBD, opportunity trees
  • strategy & positioning — the bet, the segment, the frame
  • definition — PRDs & checkable acceptance criteria
  • prioritization — multi-engine ranking & outcome roadmaps
  • measures of success — north-star & metric trees
  • the risk register — a living assumption & evidence ledger

hands off to

contribute to; own none

  • design — experience, UI, states, IA
  • frontend — production client code
  • architecture — system & technical decisions
  • marketing — demand, GTM, market copy
  • growth — funnel experiments & loops
  • sales · success — qualification/CRM · onboarding, adoption

Own the decision about what to build; not its execution. When upstream artifacts (research, a founder brief, analytics) are supplied, product uses them without silently overriding decisions already made.

The faceted router

SKILL.md is a router, not a script. Every request selects the smallest sufficient route: one primary job — the nine below — combined with at most one surface overlay that reshapes discovery, metrics, and the PRD for the product type. Read the selected references completely; load nothing unrelated. The nine jobs and five surfaces compose into 9 × 5 = 45 routes — from a two-file read to a full front-of-lifecycle pass — without ever reading all fifteen references at once.

Five surface overlays

One overlay, at most, reshapes the job for the product type — the same PRD is scoped differently for a marketing site than a SaaS app. Agentic is the first-class differentiator, expanded below.

Website surface-website.md

Marketing / content site: the "product" is a positioning-and-conversion artifact. Proof strategy by vertical & stakes; E-E-A-T and credibility layers as requirements.

lensConversion rate · lead quality · Attention Ratio

App & SaaS surface-app-saas.md

Accounts & recurring usage: revenue is a lifecycle, not a conversion. Onboarding and the core loop are first-class PRD scope; churn is a discovery question.

lensActivation · retention · MRR / churn tree

Mobile surface-mobile.md

Store distribution, ASO framing, and platform permissions as product constraints; short, interruption-driven sessions frame the jobs.

lensInstall→activation · store health · permissions

Agentic / AI differentiator

The model acts under uncertainty. Eval-as-acceptance-criteria, capability & failure-mode mapping, human-in-the-loop autonomy, and model choice as a product decision.

lensEval pass-rate · escalation · cost-per-task

Internal / enterprise surface-internal.md

Built for employees, not customers: adoption replaces acquisition, the buyer isn't the user, and operational cost drives prioritization.

lensAdoption rate · task-time · error reduction

The agentic wedge

Nearly no PM discipline covers products whose value comes from a model acting, not a deterministic feature wrapped around a model call. For an agentic feature, desirability and feasibility collapse — a job is desirable only if a model can do it reliably, and reliability is empirical. So the headline move:

The eval set is the acceptance criteria — not a QA afterthought.

Registered in the PRD, gating launch

# productspec-ai-evals — an acceptance criterion, re-checked
# every time the model or prompt changes
id: EVAL-1
job: draft a support reply from the ticket thread
task_set: 40 real transcripts, not invented cases
evaluator: llm_judge  # state the judge's own error rate
pass_threshold: ≥ 90% · 0 hallucination-flagged
offline_eval: fixed set, before every release
online_eval: sampled prod traffic — catches drift
regression_gate: block release if it regresses

Capability tier per job (§ map before a PRD line)

  • Reliableclears the bar with no scaffolding → candidate for act
  • Assistableneeds retrieval/tools → ship the scaffolding as a hard dependency
  • Unreliablefails even scaffolded → don't ship the job agentically

Autonomy by reversibility & blast radius

  • Suggestproposes; acts only on explicit user action
  • Draftfull artifact for the user to edit/send
  • Act-with-approvalexecutes after one confirmation
  • Actno per-action confirmation — reversible, low blast radius
The sharp distinction: eval thresholds that gate a release live in acceptance criteria (pre-launch, pass/fail) — never in success metrics (post-launch outcome tracking). The same score can feed both; conflating them lets a team ship on a number that was never a gate. Failure modes are first-class product concerns, each mapped to an eval case or a risk-register entry:
hallucinationwrong-tool selectionsilent errorlatency spikescontext poisoning / drift

The universal invariants

Six rules govern every route, whichever references it loads — the load-bearing ones, condensed.

  • Discovery before solutionFrame opportunities, not features. Never let a stakeholder design the solution before the problem is validated; run the cheapest probe that tells the harshest truth.
  • Label the evidenceTag every claim Fact / Inference / Assumption. Assumptions flow to the risk register, never into a fabricated fact.
  • Calibrated confidenceMarket sizes and targets are ranges with a source tier, never a false point. TAM is a ceiling; derive revenue from SOM.
  • Prioritization is multi-engineRun every applicable framework, report the divergence as the finding, and refuse to fabricate missing inputs to force one rank.
  • Outcomes over outputsA metric, a %, a timeframe, a named segment — never "great results" or "everyone." Specificity over superlatives.
  • State the surface and the registerName the product type; recommend — never silently invoke — the sibling skills for work product doesn't own. Every artifact records its objective, evidence split, decisions, success criteria, and open questions.

The handoff seam

When downstream build work is expected, product emits a compact handoff.yaml beside the human artifact — a routing index into the PRD, strategy, or metric tree, never a copy of it. Standalone by default; the companion appears only in pipeline mode, when a consumer will actually read it.

product decides

skill: product
status: complete
objective: ""   # only required field
artifacts_created: [prd, metric-tree]
decisions: · assumptions: · constraints:
risks: [R-01, R-04]  # pointers, not the register
validation: performed / remaining
recommended_next: design · architecture
→→

design

Validated problem, target segment, the success metrics to design toward, hard constraints, and the open questions it must not silently resolve.

architecture

Non-functional requirements, constraints, technical-feasibility risks, and acceptance criteria that gate technical decisions — an agentic eval threshold constrains model choice.

Seam discipline. The human artifact is the real deliverable; if a consumer needs more than the index gives, the artifact itself is incomplete — the yaml never grows to compensate. Every downstream skill tolerates every field but objective being absent.
